Today, **Barolingo Korean Premium** went live on Google Play.

The app for global K-pop and K-drama fans learning Korean the most natural way — through the songs and dramas you already love.

## Premium benefits

### 1. Ad-free experience
Focus on learning. Ads disappear from session complete screens, friend tabs, and the home banner.

### 2. Unlimited friends
Free accounts cap at 5 friends. Premium = unlimited. Connect with global Korean learners, send cheers and messages.

### 3. Unlimited K-Challenge
Free users get 3 daily Wordle K-Challenges. Premium = unlimited tries.

### 4. Exclusive K-content series
- **K-pop ballad phrases** — longing, euphoria, confidence, cheering, memories
- **K-drama iconic scenes** — confession, farewell, reconciliation, reunion, promise

> No direct lyric/dialogue quotation. General Korean phrases + K-content context (copyright safe).

## Pricing

Monthly and yearly plans available. Google Play billing (auto-renew monthly, cancel anytime in Play Store).

iOS coming soon (early June).
